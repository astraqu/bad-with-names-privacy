Privacy Policy

Overview

Marbls runs on your iPhone. There's no account to create, no server this app talks to, and no third-party analytics or crash-reporting code in it. The people, circles, places, photos, attachments, and voice transcripts you add are stored only in this app's local database on your phone.

What This App Handles

Everything the app stores is typed or captured by you: the people you add and what you record about them (name, notes, traits, text transcribed from your voice, photos, and key dates), the circles and places you organize them into, and any contact or document you choose to link. The app has no way to check any of it and doesn't try to.

On-Device Processing

You can talk to the app in two places: the Capture tab, and the mic on a person's profile that adds details to that person. In both, Apple's speech recognition turns your voice into text on this device (SpeechAnalyzer on iOS 26, with Apple's older recognizer as a backup, also set to on-device only). Apple Intelligence then reads that text on this device to fill in the person's details. The audio isn't saved, and none of the audio or text is uploaded anywhere. The first time, iOS may download Apple's speech model. iOS handles that download, and it contains none of your data.

Apple Intelligence

Voice capture, and the details it fills in for you, use Apple Intelligence through Apple's on-device model. The app doesn't send this to Apple or any other server. These features only turn on for iPhones that support Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer), with Apple Intelligence turned on and the iPhone and Siri set to English. On other iPhones, voice capture stays off: the Capture tab explains what's needed, the profile mic doesn't appear, and you add and edit people by typing.

Permissions

Microphone

Used only while you tap a mic and talk about someone, in Capture or on a person's profile, so your voice can be turned into text. Audio is processed on this device and isn't saved. Only asked for on iPhones where voice capture is available.

Speech Recognition

Used to turn what you say into text, so you don't have to type. iOS asks for this before the app can use speech recognition. The app keeps recognition on this device.

Contacts

Used only if you choose to link a person's profile to one of your existing contacts, so the app can show that contact's info and let you jump to it. The app never edits your address book.

Camera

Used only if you scan a document to attach to a person's profile. Scans stay on your device.

Photos

The app doesn't ask for access to your photo library. When you add a photo to a person, circle, or place, or attach a picture, you choose it in Apple's photo picker, and only the photos you pick are given to the app. They are stored in the app's local database on your phone.

Face ID

Used only if you lock an individual person's profile, so that profile can't be opened without Face ID or your device passcode. This does not encrypt the underlying data.

Notifications

Used for optional reminders: a weekly nudge about people you haven't spoken to in a while (Settings > Reconnect Reminders), and reminders for key dates you add to a person. These are scheduled and delivered on this device. Nothing is sent through a push service.

Places and Location

This app never asks for your location and has no background or when-in-use location access. When you search for a place to pin (Places), the text you type is sent to Apple's Maps service to find matching addresses and points of interest. This is the same lookup Apple's own Maps app does for any search. No name, profile, or other information from this app is attached to that search, only the search text itself. The Places map is drawn by Apple Maps, which loads map images for the area on screen from Apple, as any map in any app does.

Sharing the App

Settings > Share Marbls shows a QR code and a Share link button for the app's App Store page. The QR code is made on your device. Share link opens the iOS share sheet with only the App Store link, and you choose where it goes. Settings > Rate on the App Store opens the App Store. The app itself sends nothing.

No Sharing or Selling

This app doesn't sell, rent, trade, or share your data with anyone. Apart from the Apple Maps requests above, it has no way to send your data anywhere. Some things leave your phone only when you choose to send them: a file you export, an attachment you share, the App Store link, or an email you write from Settings > Contact / Feedback or Feature Requests. That email opens as a draft in your own mail app and includes the app version, iPhone model, and iOS version, which you can delete before sending. There's no ad network and no analytics.

Your Control

Settings > Export My Data saves your people, circles, places, and their details as a file, in a place you choose. Photos and attachments aren't included in the export yet. Settings > Import Data replaces everything on this device with the contents of an exported file. Settings > Erase All Data deletes every person, circle, and place on this device. Deleting the app removes its local database too.

Children

This app isn't directed at children, collects nothing beyond what's described above, and has no account system or targeted advertising of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

If how this app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated to describe the new behavior before that change ships.
