Support

Marbls is a private, on-device app for remembering the people you meet.

Contact

For help, questions, or bug reports, email astraqudevsupport@gmail.com. You can also use Settings > Contact / Feedback in the app.

Frequently Asked

Is my data private?

Yes. The people, circles, places, and connections you save stay on your device. There's no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics, and the app doesn't collect or share your data. Voice capture runs on your device too. The one exception is Places: the text of a place search goes to Apple Maps to find the address, and the map itself is loaded from Apple Maps.

What do I need for voice capture?

Voice capture uses Apple Intelligence, so it needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer with Apple Intelligence turned on, and your iPhone and Siri set to English. On other iPhones you can still add and edit people by typing. If your iPhone supports it but Capture says it isn't ready, follow the steps it shows. Capture turns on by itself once everything is ready.

How do I add someone?

Open the Capture tab, tap the mic, and talk naturally about the person you just met. The app transcribes it on your device, then either adds the details to someone you've already saved or starts a new profile. You can also tap + in the People tab and type.

How do I add details to someone I already saved?

Open them from the People tab and tap Edit / Add at the top of their profile. If voice capture is available on your iPhone, you can also tap the mic on their profile and say what you learned.

How do I share the app with a friend?

Go to Settings > Share Marbls. Your friend can scan the QR code with their camera, or you can tap Share link to send the App Store link.

How do I back up or delete my data?

Go to Settings in the app. Export My Data saves a copy of your people, circles, and places as a file (photos and attachments aren't included yet). Import Data restores from that file. Erase All Data removes everything from your device. Deleting the app also removes its data.
