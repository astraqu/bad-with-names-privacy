Frequently Asked

Is my data private?

Yes. The people, circles, places, and connections you save stay on your device. There's no account, no cloud sync, and no analytics, and the app doesn't collect or share your data. The one exception is a place search, where only the text you type goes to Apple Maps to find the address.

How do I add someone?

Open the Capture tab, tap the mic, and talk naturally about the person you just met. The app transcribes it on your device, then either adds the details to someone you've already saved or starts a new profile.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings in the app. Export My Data saves a copy of everything as a file, and Erase All Data removes everything from your device. Deleting the app also removes its data.
